HELP BEFORE YOU ORDER

Frequently asked questions

Why can Bilona ghee have a grainy texture?

Ghee can naturally develop a granular texture as it cools. Texture varies with preparation and storage conditions; it is not, by itself, proof of purity.

Is A2 Bilona ghee suitable for someone with lactose sensitivity?

Ghee generally contains very little milk protein or lactose, but traces may remain and individual tolerance varies. It is not a treatment or a guarantee of safety for milk allergy. Ask your clinician if you have an allergy or medical concern.

How should I store ghee?

Keep it in a clean, dry, airtight container, away from moisture and direct sunlight. Follow the storage guidance on the product label.

How quickly do you deliver to Delhi NCR or elsewhere in India?

Dispatch and delivery timelines depend on stock, destination and courier service. Share your PIN code with the farm for a current estimate.

Can I pause a monthly ghee plan?

The website collects a recurring-order enquiry only. No automatic subscription payment is created. Pause, resume and delivery dates are confirmed with the farm over WhatsApp.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes, you can enquire about a farm visit or Bhunga stay. Dates and facilities must be confirmed directly with the team.
