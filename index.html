ZERO-WASTE PHILOSOPHY

The Cow as a Coconut

Like the sacred coconut tree where every part serves life, our Pilani ecosystem operates on a closed-loop circular cycle.

1

A2 Nourishment

Calves feed first. Surplus Tharparkar A2 milk is cultured into curd and hand-churned via the Vedic Bilona method.

2

Biogas & Soil

Cow dung fuels our biogas plant for clean energy; nutrient-rich slurry feeds earthworms to create Pure Kechua Khad.

3

Sacred Dhoop

Sun-dried dung is blended with natural guggal, herbs, and temple florals to craft chemical-free Tharparkar Dhoop.
