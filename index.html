Zero-Waste Philosophy

The Cow as a Coconut

Like the coconut tree where every part is utilized, our farm operates on an ancient circular model. Absolutely nothing goes to waste.

1

Nourishment

Our Tharparkar cows produce ethical A2 milk, hand-churned into premium Bilona Ghee for your family's health.

2

Energy & Soil

Manure fuels our biogas plant to power the farm. The resulting slurry feeds earthworms, creating Pure Kechua Khad.

3

Spirituality

Any remaining dung is blended with botanical herbs and temple florals to craft chemical-free Tharparkar Dhoop.
